What it does

Lightweight, transparent, and always on your desktop. It lives in the system tray and stays out of your way.

Your favorite avatar

Load the VRM avatar you love and see it come to life, rendered with a transparent background and no window frame.

Walks your desktop

Your avatar strolls along your screen, right on top of your windows and wallpaper.

AI and agents with free MODs

Add free MODs to chat with an AI or let it operate as an agent for you.

Open my notes, please.Sure. Opening them now.

Many at once

Let several avatars walk together. Build a little crowd on your desktop.

Automatic memory release

Kuruminha frees memory by itself to stay light. Prefer to manage it yourself? Turn it off any time.

Multilingual

English, Russian and Japanese. It follows your system language by default.

Getting started

  1. Download and extract the archive.
  2. Run Kuruminha. It appears in the system tray.
  3. Right-click the tray icon, open Settings and choose your avatar.
